## What I build

I work with Claude/LLM APIs daily — agentic tool-use loops, RAG pipelines, embedding
fine-tuning, and prompt systems shipped to real users, not just notebooks.

- **[dental-ai-receptionist](https://github.com/radmirbambo/dental-ai-receptionist)** — voice AI
  administrator for dental clinics, our 2026 startup: answers calls and books visits.
  Voximplant telephony, Yandex ASR/TTS, LLM function calling, FastAPI dashboard. Working prototype.
- **[mystery-shopper](https://github.com/radmirbambo/mystery-shopper)** — agent that left leads at
  56 Moscow dental clinics and measured how fast they call back: 20 did, 10 within 5 minutes.
  Playwright form bot, FastAPI backend, Telegram operator bot, local Whisper call analysis.
  Its data moved the startup from web leads to inbound calls.
- **[pitch-coach](https://github.com/radmirbambo/pitch-coach)** — AI copilot that diagnoses
  startup pitches. Fine-tuned retrieval embeddings (Recall@1 +10.7% over base), RAG grounded
  in real historical pitch decks, Streamlit UI.
- **[invite-bot](https://github.com/radmirbambo/invite-bot)** — Telegram agent that researches
  a person on the web and drafts personalized outreach in your voice. Claude tool-use loop,
  in production for the MIPT Business Club.
- **Job Application Agent** — parses a job posting, matches it against a skill profile, and
  generates a tailored application. Agentic loop with a role-type detector, used for real
  applications.
- **[podkast-llm](https://github.com/radmirbambo/podkast-llm)** — Telegram bot that recommends
  podcasts via RAG over a Yandex Music catalog, with voice input.
